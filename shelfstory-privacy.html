Privacy Policy and Data Management for "Shelf Story: Book Match"

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the "Shelf Story: Book Match" mobile game collects, uses and manages user data.

1. Data We Collect

The game processes only the limited data needed to provide and improve it. It has no accounts of its own, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

  • Local game data: Your levels, stars, coins, lives, helpers, bookshop items, sticker album, loot collection, owl costumes, chest looks and settings are stored on your device. We have no remote access to this data.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: If you sign in with Google and turn on Google Drive backup in Settings, your save is stored in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (appDataFolder). Only this game can access that folder. The game asks for access to that folder only and cannot see your other Drive files. Your account name and email address are kept on your device only to show which account is connected in Settings. The backup is never sent to our servers. You can turn it off or sign out at any time in Settings.
  • Google Play Games (leaderboard): If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your highest cleared level is submitted to the leaderboard. Google uses your Play Games player profile (gamer name and player ID) for this.
  • Usage and analytics data (Firebase Analytics): We use Google's Firebase Analytics to improve the game. It may collect:
    • in-game events, such as levels played, won or lost, helper use, reward and loot openings, notification interactions and shop interactions;
    • an app instance ID;
    • technical information, such as device model, OS version, language and approximate country.
    This data does not directly identify you, and we review it only as aggregate statistics.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob and Unity Ads): The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and some ads may be served by Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. These services may collect data such as your device's Advertising ID to serve and measure ads. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent to personalized ads through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Each provider's own privacy policy governs how it processes this data.
  • Purchase data (Google Play Billing): Google Play handles all in-app purchases. We never see or store your card or payment details. The game only checks which product was purchased so it can deliver it.
  • Notifications: The game creates reminders locally on your device: lives refilled, daily chest ready, special offer ending, and come back and play. No data is sent to a server for them. On Android 13 and later, they appear only if you allow notifications. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in your phone's settings.
  • App updates and reviews: The game uses Google Play's in-app update service to check for new versions, and Google Play's in-app review prompt so you can rate the game. Google Play handles both, and we cannot see the review you write.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide the game and save your progress on your device (and, if you choose, in your Google Drive).
  • To balance level difficulty, find bugs and improve the game.
  • To support the game's development and maintenance through in-app ads.
  • To deliver the products you purchase.
  • To provide the leaderboard and reminder notifications.

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the service providers listed below.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing and in-app updates/reviews — Google Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Google and Google Drive (optional backup) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP) — AdMob and user data
  • Unity Ads — Unity Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — Privacy and Security in Firebase

4. Data Security

Game data on your device is protected by your operating system's standard security. The Google Drive backup is protected by Google's own security. Data sent between the game and Google or ad services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Data Deletion Policy and Instructions

In line with Google Play policies, every user has the right to delete their data. The game has no server-side accounts, so "deleting your account" means permanently deleting all game data on your device and, if you used it, your Drive backup.

To delete the game data on your device:

  1. Open Settings → Apps on your phone.
  2. Select "Shelf Story: Book Match".
  3. Open Storage and tap "Clear data" (or "Clear storage").
  4. After you confirm, your levels, coins, collections, settings and all other game data are permanently deleted, and the game starts as if newly installed. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

To delete your Google Drive backup: On a computer, open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps, find "Shelf Story: Book Match" and choose Options → Delete hidden app data. You can also sign out in the game under Settings → Google Drive backup, and remove the game's access on your Google Account third-party connections page.

Leaderboard data: You can manage or delete your Google Play Games profile and game activity in Google Play Games settings.

Analytics and advertising data: Data collected by Firebase Analytics, AdMob and Unity Ads is kept according to each provider's own retention periods and does not directly identify you. To ask for its deletion, email mobileappkulaksiz54@gmail.com with your game language and device model. We handle requests within 30 days. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Important: Deleted game data and purchased virtual items (coins, helpers, costumes) cannot be recovered, unless you made a Drive backup before deleting and restore it in the game. One-time purchases such as "Remove Ads" are restored automatically through Google Play when you open the game with the same Google account.

6. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. We recommend checking this page regularly.
