Privacy Policy and Data Management for "Plumbloom"

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the "Plumbloom: Pipe Connect Puzzle" mobile app collects, uses and manages user data.

1. Data We Collect

The app has no accounts of its own. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

  • Local game data: Your level, stars, coins, lives, helpers, themes, daily reward and quest progress, and settings are stored on your device. We have no remote access to this data.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: If you turn on Google Drive backup in Settings, your save is stored in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (appDataFolder) that only this app can access. The app requests access to that folder only and cannot see your other Drive files. The backup is never sent to our servers. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Google Play Games (leaderboard): If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your highest cleared level is submitted to the leaderboard. Google uses your Play Games player profile (gamer name and player ID) for this.
  • Usage and analytics data (Firebase Analytics): To improve the game we use Google's Firebase Analytics. It may collect in-game events (levels played, won or lost, helper use, shop interactions), an app instance ID and technical information such as device model, OS version, language and approximate country. This data does not directly identify you and is reviewed only as aggregate statistics.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob and Unity Ads): The app shows ads through Google AdMob; some ads may be served by Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. These services may collect data such as your device's Advertising ID to serve and measure ads. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent for personalized ads through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); you can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. This processing is governed by each provider's privacy policy.
  • Purchase data (Google Play Billing): In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details; the app only verifies which product was purchased in order to deliver it.
  • Notifications: Reminders (lives refilled, daily reward and quests ready, come back and play) are created locally on your device. No data is sent to a server for them. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in your phone's settings.
  • Bug reports (optional): When you tap Settings → Report a bug, your phone's email app opens with the app name, app version, device brand and model, Android version and language added to the message. This information reaches us only if you send the email yourself, and you can review or remove it before sending. We use your email address and message only to reply to you and fix the problem.
  • Sharing and links: Share with friends opens your phone's standard share menu; we cannot see who you share with or which app you use. Rate us and Our other games open Google Play, where Google's privacy policy applies.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide the game and save your progress on your device (and, if you choose, in your Google Drive).
  • To balance level difficulty, find bugs and improve the game.
  • To support the app's development and maintenance through in-app ads.
  • To deliver the products you purchase.
  • To provide the leaderboard and reminder notifications.
  • To answer the bug reports and questions you send us.

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed below.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services, Google Play Games and Google Play Billing — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Drive (optional backup) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — AdMob and user data
  • Unity Ads — Unity Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — Privacy and Security in Firebase

4. Data Security

Game data on your device is protected by your operating system's standard security. Data sent between the app and Google or ad services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Data Deletion Policy and Instructions

In line with Google Play policies, every user has the right to delete their data. Because the app does not use server-side accounts, "deleting your account" means permanently deleting all game data on your device and, if used, your Drive backup.

To delete the game data on your device:

  1. Open Settings → Apps on your phone.
  2. Select "Plumbloom".
  3. Open Storage and tap "Clear data" (or "Clear storage").
  4. Once confirmed, your level, coins, settings and all other game data are permanently deleted. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

To delete your Google Drive backup: On a computer, open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps, find "Plumbloom" and choose Options → Delete hidden app data. You can also disconnect Drive in the app's Settings.

Leaderboard data: You can manage or delete your Google Play Games profile and game activity in Google Play Games settings.

Analytics and advertising data: Data collected by Firebase Analytics, AdMob and Unity Ads is subject to those providers' retention periods and does not directly identify you. To request its deletion, email mobileappkulaksiz54@gmail.com with your app language and device model; requests are handled within 30 days. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Bug report emails: To have the emails you sent us deleted, just write to the same address; they will be deleted within 30 days.

Important: Deleted game data and purchased virtual items (coins, helpers, unlimited lives) cannot be recovered. One-time purchases such as "Remove ads" are restored automatically through Google Play when you open the app with the same Google account.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.
